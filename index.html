Best with a controller PS4, PS5, Xbox and most Bluetooth controllers work. The game switches to controller buttons as soon as you press one.
How to pair a controller with your iPhone
  1. PS5: hold Create + PS until the light bar flashes. PS4: hold Share + PS.
  2. Xbox: hold the small pair button on top until the Xbox logo flashes fast.
  3. On the iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth, then tap the controller under Other Devices.
  4. Come back here and press any button. Touch controls come back whenever you touch the screen.

The AI sealed the big cities. Everyone came home to the small towns, and Opelika runs on lanterns, trade and whatever falls off the machines' freight trains. The courthouse square is a market again. The tent city by the tracks is the one place nobody fights.

Watch for the train. When it breaks down on the tracks, race the machines and the Railside Crew for the crates. Take buildings, collect rent, keep your stash in the tent city lockers. Every business has a made-up name.
